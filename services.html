BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few good
questions.

How do you price a project?

I quote each project based on scope, deliverables, production needs and timing. Share your brief and a budget range so we can find an appropriate approach. The scope and fee are agreed before work begins.

Can I hire you for one part of a project?

Yes. We can focus on a defined design task, an edit, a visual direction or a specific deliverable. I can also help connect several pieces when the project needs a broader creative approach.

What do you need to get started?

A short overview of your goal, audience, preferred timing and budget is a useful starting point. Existing brand assets, relevant references and any content you already have help shape the direction.

How are revisions handled?

We agree on review stages and revision rounds in the project scope. Feedback works best when it is consolidated and connected to the goal. Additional work or changes to the brief are discussed before proceeding.

Can we collaborate remotely?

Design, planning, editing and feedback can be handled remotely. If a project needs filming or photography on location, we discuss the location, production requirements and associated costs in the scope.

What do I receive at the end?

You receive the agreed deliverables in organized, usable formats. Source files, export versions, usage guidance, licensing and ownership terms are specified in the project agreement so the handoff is clear.
