ALI REZAIE / DESIGNER & FILMMAKER
THE APPROACH

Purpose first.
Craft throughout.

I’m Ali Rezaie, a visual designer and filmmaker working across graphic design and video production.

I’m interested in the way a visual system creates recognition and a moving image creates feeling. Bringing the two together gives a story more ways to connect.

My work is shaped by a clear idea, cinematic attention to framing and a modern, adaptable visual language. Supporting skills in photography, UI/UX, social media and HTML/CSS help carry that direction across touchpoints.
